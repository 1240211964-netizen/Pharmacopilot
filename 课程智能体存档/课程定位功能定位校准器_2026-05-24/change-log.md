# Change Log

## Before

- Station 1 behaved like the other one-chart-one-question stations.
- The positioning visual was an abstract triangle named `课程定位三角图`.
- The main interaction framed positioning as a generic choice question.

## After

- Station 1 is now a knowledge-point teaching-function positioning calibrator.
- The left side presents three concrete evidence cards instead of an abstract chart.
- The right side asks teachers to decide the lesson main line from those three evidence sources.
- Feedback explains the role of A, B, and C after every selection.
- The station produces a concrete positioning sentence and downstream design constraints for case, task, and evaluation design.
- The global process label now says `依据/图表` instead of forcing station 1 into a `证据图` frame.
